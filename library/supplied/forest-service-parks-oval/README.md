# Forest Service / British Columbia / Parks tree oval

Two supplied vector packages of the Forest Service / British Columbia / Parks oval and its PARKS tab, which
take the place of the atlas's R19-O01 and S26-O01 as one card with three variants:

| Variant | Drawing | Takes the place of |
|---|---|---|
| Cream backing | `first-package/forest-service-parks-patch.svg` | R19-O01 |
| Without backing | `first-package/forest-service-parks-patch-no-backing.svg` | S26-O01 |
| Bright gold | `second-package/forest-service-bc-parks.svg` | — |

- `first-package/`: rebuilt from a 401 × 498 photograph (`source/reference-photo.png`), with its
  construction script and palette. Its README is the package's own.
- `second-package/`: a separate reconstruction from a 1122 × 1402 image, brighter in its gold, sky and
  fir. Its README is the package's own; its raster exports and PDF are left out.

Both are used whole, with no compose step. The previews are the SVGs rendered in Chromium at 440 px wide.
Neither is recovered production art; the typeface, thread colours and the patch's size are interpreted.
