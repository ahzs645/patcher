# MC19-O02 — Conservation Officer olive lower rocker

Reference: [Moosecop paired cloth specimen](https://moosecop.net/History/BC_Fish_Wildlife_cloth_CO.jpg), cropped in PofBC as MC19-O02 (371 × 118 pixels). The adjacent shield is not part of this separate rocker.

The new outline is constructed with symmetric cubic Bézier curves and rounded end transitions. The 19 capital letters are original narrow filled glyphs with independent horizontal positions and tangent rotations on a shallow lower arc. The olive and gold colours are a visual interpretation of the photograph, not calibrated thread measurements. The artwork contains no raster image or live text. MC20-O02 is unchanged.

Run `node library/supplied/co-olive-lower-rocker/compose.mjs` to reproduce the SVG. The 440 × 140 transparent WebP was rendered from the SVG. A 742 × 236 local raster render confirmed 19 disconnected gold glyphs. The source and baseline were visually inspected; no registered pixel-overlap score or embroidery sew-out is claimed. The generated catalogue files are updated in this draft; full atlas re-import and browser/digitizer validation remain required before release.
